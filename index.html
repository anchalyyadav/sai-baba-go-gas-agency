Safety First

Gas Safety Tips

Your safety is our priority. Follow these simple precautions while using your LPG cylinder.

Check for Gas Leakage

If you smell gas, turn off the regulator immediately and open doors and windows for ventilation.

Keep Cylinder Upright

Always keep the LPG cylinder upright and stable on a flat and safe surface.

Keep Away from Heat

Keep the cylinder, regulator and gas pipe away from direct flames and excessive heat.

Check the Gas Hose

Check the gas hose regularly and replace it if you notice cracks, damage or signs of wear.

Avoid Electrical Switches

If you suspect a gas leak, do not switch electrical appliances or switches on or off.

Get Help Immediately

In case of a serious gas leak or emergency, move to a safe place and contact the appropriate emergency service or gas agency.

Important Safety Reminder

Never try to repair a damaged LPG cylinder, regulator or gas connection yourself. Contact a qualified professional for assistance.
